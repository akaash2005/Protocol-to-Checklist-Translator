Cardiac rehab, phase 2, 6 weeks post-MI, cleared by cardiologist for supervised exercise.

Warm-up 5 minutes light cycling. Aerobic training at 60-70% of heart rate reserve, target zone
110-125 bpm, 20-30 minutes, using treadmill or recumbent bike. No resistance training above 40% 1RM
for the first 4 weeks of the program.

Continuous heart rate monitoring required during every session. Rate of perceived exertion should
stay between 11-13 on the Borg scale.

Stop exercise immediately and escalate to physician if: chest pain or pressure, dizziness or
lightheadedness, heart rate exceeds 130 bpm, or SpO2 drops below 92%.

Weight and resting heart rate logged at start of each session. Cardiac rehab coordinator to review
ECG trend monthly.
