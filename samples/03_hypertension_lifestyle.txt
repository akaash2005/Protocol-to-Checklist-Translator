Hypertension, stage 1, on no medication yet -- lifestyle trial for 90 days per physician.

Sodium intake target under 1500mg/day. DASH-style eating pattern, emphasize potassium-rich vegetables
and fruit, limit processed food.

Aerobic exercise 150 minutes/week moderate intensity, e.g. brisk walking or cycling, spread across at
least 4 sessions. No heavy isometric lifting (e.g. max-effort deadlifts) until blood pressure is
controlled.

Check resting blood pressure before each session -- if systolic is above 180 or diastolic above 110,
do not exercise and escalate to physician same day. If systolic is between 160-180, keep intensity
light and log the reading for physician review at next visit.

Home BP log twice daily (morning and evening), review weekly with care coordinator. Limit alcohol to
no more than 1 drink/day.
