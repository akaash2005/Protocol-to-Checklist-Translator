Post-op right total knee replacement, week 3 post-surgery.

Program: light resistance band work only for quadriceps and hamstrings, 2 sets of 12 reps, no more
than 20 minutes total. Avoid full weight-bearing lunges and deep squats past 90 degrees until week 6.
Stationary cycling with no resistance is fine for 10 minutes as a warm-up.

Watch for joint swelling, redness, or warmth around the incision, or pain above 4/10 during or after
exercise -- if present, stop the session and escalate to the physician same day.

Protein target 1.2g/kg bodyweight per day to support healing; check in on intake weekly.

Reassess range of motion weekly using goniometer, target 90 degrees flexion by week 4.

Ice the knee for 15 minutes after each session.
