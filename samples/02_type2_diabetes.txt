Type 2 diabetes management, newly enrolled member, BMI 31.

Nutrition: carbohydrate intake capped at 45g per meal, 3 meals + 1 snack per day, no more than 15g
added sugar daily.

Walking 30 minutes at moderate pace, 5 days a week, can be split into two 15-minute sessions.
Resistance training twice weekly, full body, moderate intensity, avoid to-failure sets.

Check blood glucose before and after exercise sessions -- if pre-exercise reading is below 100 mg/dL,
give a 15g carb snack before starting; if above 250 mg/dL, skip exercise that session and notify care
coordinator. Any reading below 70 mg/dL or above 300 mg/dL at any time should be escalated to the
physician immediately.

HbA1c re-check at 12 weeks. Weight check-in weekly.
